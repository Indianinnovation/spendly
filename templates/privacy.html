Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you give us when you create an account, such as your name and email address, and the expense details you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect payment card details or access your bank accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to show you summaries of your spending. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and kept for as long as your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data deleted, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
